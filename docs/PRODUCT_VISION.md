# Product vision

LifeOS Personal helps a user understand and improve daily life through tracking, planning, goals and evidence-based insights. It is an existing application, not a greenfield project. Recommendations must be optional, grounded in recorded evidence and distinguish observed, estimated and unknown information.

The six primary sections are Today (daily control centre), Track (recording and history), Plan (commitments and tasks), Goals (targets and habits), Insights (analysis), and My Life (personal development). The user has approved Today Concept A+ and Track Concept A; protect their design and working behavior. Compact mobile presentation, accessible controls, restrained colors and minimal unnecessary scrolling are priorities.

Development proceeds one major section at a time. Finish and obtain focused acceptance for critical Today work before major Plan redesign. Substantial new concepts require the user's visual approval. No score substitutes for correctness, accessibility or data safety.
