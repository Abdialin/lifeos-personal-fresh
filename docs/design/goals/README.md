The user-supplied V1 targets are in [`release-v1/04_Goals`](../release-v1/04_Goals/), including Overview, Improve, History and supporting screens. The updated Overview uses full-width rows, category icons and a light palette, closer to the reference's row structure. Its header, weekly summary, card composition, milestones and goal-status lifecycle still differ from the target; no unverified milestone, on-track or at-risk figure is invented. A My Life activity-intelligence panel no longer appears below Goals, and remains available in My Life. Improve and History still use two-column cards and retain dark cards in light mode, although their light-page headings were made readable. The target-evidence and row changes are incremental rather than a complete Goals redesign. Visual, functional, device and user acceptance remain open.
